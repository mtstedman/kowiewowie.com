Slow preview / experimental. May be very slow; full-game correctness is not established.

Loading translated game… First load may take a minute. Reset is available while loading.

Keyboard: arrows, Z = A, X = B, Shift = Select. Rendering starts automatically after loading and Reset unless paused or hidden. Audio stays muted until a user gesture. Game Start / Enter presses controller Start and enables available audio even while rendering; it also resumes after pause. Engine resume resumes rendering and enables available audio without pressing controller Start. Frames may take up to a minute of processing; frame and input-release responses allow two minutes. Initialization has no time limit. Reset terminates the current worker and starts a fresh load. Quick taps wait for a frame; repeated taps play in order. Overlapping controls hold a button until all are released. Input buffering is limited: a full buffer stops the preview and asks for Reset. Pause, Reset, focus loss and hiding the page clear all input.

Experimental hardware integration. Unsupported operations stop with a visible error.
